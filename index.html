How to read this dashboard

Glossary

Contracted revenue
The freight fee agreed before the blockade. It does not change with the route used.
Revenue recognized
Contracted revenue that has been earned. It is $0 for shipments still Held in Gulf.
Cost to serve
Freight + fuel + war-risk insurance + late-delivery penalties.
Gross margin
Revenue recognized minus cost to serve. Negative means a loss.
DIFOT
Delivered In Full, On Time — the share of shipments that met the contract's delivery promise.
Energy cargo
Crude oil and refined petrochemicals, shipped in bulk tankers.

Data treatments & assumptions

  • All 243 shipments are included (5 Jan – 22 Mar 2026). Shipment IDs have no duplicates.
  • Two lower-case region values were standardised (“east asia”, “europe”).
  • Two shipments (SGL-1185, SGL-1212) had a blank insurance cost. It was filled in as total cost minus freight, fuel and penalty, so their totals and margins are unchanged.
  • Direct (Pre-Blockade) shipments ran 5–31 Jan with the same customers and contracts, so they serve as the cost baseline.
  • Margin % is shown only as a check. The dataset notes it looks extreme for Air Bridge and Overland Truck because those were priced as emergency substitutes.
  • Routes were chosen, not assigned at random, so differences between routes also reflect the cargo put on them.
